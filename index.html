how to play

Guess the memecoin of the day in 6 tries. Every guess reveals how close you are on five axes:

  • Chain — where it launched. yellow = both EVM chains.
  • Type — the mascot: dog, cat, frog, AI, politics… yellow = same family (e.g. dog vs cat).
  • Year — launch year. yellow = within 1 year. ▲▼ point to the answer.
  • Peak — peak market cap, judged by order of magnitude (<$10M, $10M+, $100M+, $1B+, $10B+).
  • Fate — Icon · Alive · Faded · Collapsed · Scandal. yellow = same side of the dirt.
