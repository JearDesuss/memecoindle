The daily pot

Every day, 100% of the token's trading fees become that day's pot. It pays out at 00:00 UTC — the board as it stands at that moment is the board that gets paid.

  • Three boards, three pots — the day's fees split evenly across Classic, Blur and Lore, so a Lore win pays what a Classic win pays.
  • The top ten are in — and the slices are ranked: ten parts at #1, nine at #2, down to one part at #10.
  • A slice needs a wallet — paste an address into Settings. Whatever is on file at 00:00 UTC is where your slice goes.
  • Nothing is held back — a ranked player with no wallet drops out of the split, and their parts go pro-rata to the ranked players who have one. An unclaimed slice is not carried over to tomorrow; it is paid out today, to someone else.

Solana or EVM address, whichever chain the token trades on. Nothing here verifies an address — an address typed wrong is a payment sent wrong, so paste it.
