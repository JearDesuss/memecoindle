The daily pot

100% of the token's trading fees go into that day's pot. It pays out at 00:00 UTC to whoever is on the board at that moment.

  • The fees split evenly across Classic, Blur and Lore, so a win in any mode is worth the same.
  • The top ten on each board get paid by rank: ten shares for #1, nine for #2, down to one for #10.
  • You need a wallet address in Settings to get paid. The payout goes to whatever is on file at 00:00 UTC.
  • If a ranked player has no wallet, their share goes to the ranked players who do, split the same way. Nothing rolls over to the next day.

Use a Solana or EVM address, whichever chain the token trades on. Nothing here checks it, so paste it. A typo sends the payment somewhere else.
